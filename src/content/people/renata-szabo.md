Renata spent eleven years auditing restaurant groups before their books ever
became public, reading the same profit-and-loss statements a diner never
sees. She founded Closure Studies after noticing that every failed
restaurant she'd ever audited had left a public trail that told most of the
story anyway — reviews, inspection records, an abandoned delivery-app
listing — and that nobody was teaching students to read it.

She runs the weeks built on evidence you can put a number on: choosing a
viable specimen, the location audit, reading a menu as financial
disclosure, and testing the landlord's-fault story everyone reaches for
first. Office hours are by email; there is no drop-in slot, because a
course about evidence should run on scheduled appointments, not ambient
availability.
