## Before the session

Find the fullest version of your specimen's menu you can — a cached page,
a delivery-app archive, photos in old reviews. Price out the ingredients of
three dishes as best you can and work out roughly what share of the sale
price they'd eat.

## In the session

A long menu with cheap-looking prices is usually a kitchen trying to be
everything to everyone, and that shows up in cost percentages before it
shows up in reviews — Renata walks through why, then you apply it to your
own specimen and flag anything that doesn't fit the pattern, which is
usually the more interesting case.

## Afterwards

The Specimen Dossier is due: your case file, your location audit and your
menu reading, assembled into the first checkpoint the course marks. It
doesn't need a theory of the closure yet — it needs to prove you have
enough real material to build one.
