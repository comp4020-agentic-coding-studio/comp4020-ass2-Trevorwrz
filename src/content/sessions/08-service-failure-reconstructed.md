## Before the session

Find at least two reviews, ideally from different periods, that describe
the same kind of service problem — slow tables, a specific staffing gap, an
ownership change reviewers noticed before it was announced. One account is
an anecdote; two independent ones start to be a pattern.

## In the session

Desmond treats this like witness cross-referencing: where accounts agree,
where they conflict, and what a conflict tells you (two people at the same
table rarely disagree about whether food arrived — they do disagree about
whether it was worth the wait). You reconstruct your specimen's service
timeline as a group exercise before doing your own.

## Afterwards

A service-failure timeline built from more than one witness, ready to sit
alongside the location, menu and review-history findings — and one more
place a lazy single-cause story might not survive contact with the
evidence.
