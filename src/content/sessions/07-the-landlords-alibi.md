## Before the session

Check whether "the rent went up" (or its local equivalent — a lease
non-renewal, a redevelopment) is actually part of your specimen's story, or
whether you've been reaching for it because it's the easiest explanation
available for any closure anywhere.

## In the session

Renata sets up the test: if rent were the real cause, what would you expect
to be true elsewhere in the case file — declining margins visible in the
menu reading, a lease term that lines up with the closure date, a
landlord dispute mentioned anywhere in the reviews or news coverage? You
check your specimen against those expectations rather than against your
gut.

## Afterwards

A verdict on the landlord's alibi — guilty, exonerated, or genuinely
unknown from the available evidence — which is itself a legitimate finding
for the Autopsy Report, not a failure to find an answer.
