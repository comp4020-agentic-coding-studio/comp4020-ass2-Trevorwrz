## Before the session

Attend this week's lecture on how aviation incident reports, software
post-mortems and restaurant criticism each structure a failure
investigation differently, then pick whichever framework fits your
specimen's evidence best.

## In the session

You run your specimen through the chosen framework in front of the room,
which usually means discovering a question the framework insists you
answer that you'd been quietly skipping — an incident-report method
insisting on a timeline of who-knew-what-when, say, when you'd been writing
in vague impressions.

## Afterwards

A second, differently-shaped pass at your specimen, which either confirms
what the location audit and the review timeline already suggested or
complicates it — both outcomes are useful going into next week's test of
the landlord's-fault story.
