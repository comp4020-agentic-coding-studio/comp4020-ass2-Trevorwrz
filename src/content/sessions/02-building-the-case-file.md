## Before the session

Pull everything you can find on your specimen without stopping to
interpret any of it: the last dozen reviews in date order, a health
inspection record if the jurisdiction publishes them, an archive.org
capture of the restaurant's own site or delivery-app page, any news
coverage of the opening or the closing. Desmond's rule: collect first,
theorise never, at least this week.

## In the session

You build a shared timeline format everyone in the course will use for
their own specimen, and swap case files with a partner who checks whether
each item is actually dated and actually sourced — not whether the theory
implied by the file sounds right, because there isn't meant to be one yet.

## Afterwards

A dated, sourced case file, still theory-free, that next week's location
audit and every later week can pull evidence from without re-searching from
scratch.
