## Before the session

Estimate what your specimen's site would have cost to lease, using
comparable local listings, and what revenue a venue that size and price
point would need to clear it — Renata's back-of-envelope method, not a real
audit. Map the competing restaurants within a comparable walk.

## In the session

Renata works a live example on the board first, including the two mistakes
everyone makes: comparing rent to peak-season revenue instead of an average
month, and counting "nearby" competitors by straight-line distance instead
of the route a customer would actually walk. You then run your own numbers
and defend them to a partner who is explicitly told to find the weak
assumption.

## Afterwards

A location viability verdict for your specimen, with the arithmetic
attached — the first real building block of the eventual Autopsy Report,
and the first place your gut theory about "why it closed" gets to survive
contact with a number.
