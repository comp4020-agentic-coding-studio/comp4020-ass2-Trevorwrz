## Before the session

Prepare a ten-minute case presentation: the specimen, the strongest two or
three pieces of evidence, and the verdict. Everything else you found this
semester is backup material for the questions, not part of the pitch.

## In the session

You present to the room as a jury. Renata and Desmond, and anyone else who
wants in, cross-examine: the landlord's-alibi test gets checked, the
survivorship-bias addendum from week 10 gets checked, and any claim that
sounds more confident than its source supports gets pushed on. A verdict
that survives real pushback is worth more than one that was never
challenged.

## Afterwards

A final mark on the Coroner's Inquest, and — for anyone whose specimen's
story turns out to generalise — an invitation to write it up properly for
next year's incoming cohort, since one useful thing this course can leave
behind is a growing library of restaurants that taught somebody something.
