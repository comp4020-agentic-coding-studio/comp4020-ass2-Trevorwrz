## Before the session

Finish a full draft. Not notes, not an outline — a report someone else
could read cover to cover and follow the argument for why your specimen
closed.

## In the session

You swap drafts with a partner whose only job is adversarial: find the
claim that's stated more confidently than the evidence supports, the source
that's doing more work than it should, the tidy narrative that arrived
before the evidence did. You do the same to theirs. This is the week the
report gets harder to defend, on purpose, before a marker does it for real.

## Afterwards

The Autopsy Report is due, cross-examined at least once by someone who
wasn't trying to be kind to it.
