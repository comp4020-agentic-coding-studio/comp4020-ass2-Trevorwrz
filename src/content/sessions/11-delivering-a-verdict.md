## Before the session

Rank your findings from the semester by how much weight each deserves in
the final verdict — location, menu economics, service, reviews, whatever
the landlord's-alibi week concluded. Most specimens have one dominant cause
and several minor ones; this week is about admitting which is which
instead of listing all of them as equally important.

## In the session

Renata's test for a good verdict: could a stranger opening a similar
restaurant tomorrow actually use it? "The food was inconsistent" fails that
test; "the kitchen ran a menu with forty items and a two-line staff" passes
it. You rewrite your verdict until it passes.

## Afterwards

A one-paragraph verdict, ranked and actionable, ready to be defended out
loud at the Coroner's Inquest.
