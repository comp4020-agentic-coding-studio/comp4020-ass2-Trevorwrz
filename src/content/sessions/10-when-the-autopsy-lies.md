## Before the session

Attend this week's lecture on why we only ever study restaurants that
failed and also left a trail — the ones that failed quietly, with no
reviews and no coverage, are invisible to every method this course teaches,
and that's a bias built into the whole exercise, not just your specimen.

## In the session

You re-read your own submitted Autopsy Report specifically looking for
places you picked the clip, quote or number that fit the story you'd
already decided on, rather than the one that best represented the evidence.
Desmond's provocation: if you can't find at least one, you weren't looking
hard enough.

## Afterwards

A short, honest addendum to your own thinking — not a resubmission, a
record of what you'd check differently — that feeds directly into how you
frame the verdict at the Coroner's Inquest.
