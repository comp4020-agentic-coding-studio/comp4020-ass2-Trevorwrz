## Before the session

Pull every review you can date and plot rating (or your own rough
sentiment call) against time. You're hunting for the point the line bends,
which is very rarely the same week people online started saying "I heard
this place is struggling."

## In the session

Desmond's argument: a five-star review from a regular and a one-star review
from someone who waited forty minutes carry different evidentiary weight,
and treating them as equally informative is how a lot of amateur
post-mortems go wrong. You practise weighting your own specimen's reviews
and defending the weights out loud.

## Afterwards

An inflection point with a date attached, and a first honest answer to
whether the reviews saw the ending coming before the owners did — or
whether, just as often happens, they didn't.
