## The brief

> Prove you have a real specimen and enough evidence to work with — not yet
> an explanation.

This is the checkpoint that stops the semester from being built on a
restaurant nobody can actually investigate. A strong dossier has more
sources than you think you need and resists the temptation to already know
why the place closed; that temptation is what week 9's peer
cross-examination exists to catch if you don't catch it yourself first.

## What you submit

A dossier containing: the specimen's name, address and closure date; the
sourced, dated timeline from weeks 1–2; the location-viability numbers from
week 3; and the menu cost reading from week 4. Everything traces to a
public source a marker can check.
