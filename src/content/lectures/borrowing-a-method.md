## What this covers

None of the professions that formalised post-mortem analysis were thinking
about restaurants. Aviation investigators build a timeline before a theory,
on purpose, because a first theory that arrives early captures every
investigator who hears it. Product post-mortems separate the proximate
cause from the five things that had to go wrong first for it to matter.
Clinical autopsy distinguishes what killed the patient from what merely
happened to be true at time of death.

## What it sets up

The rest of the semester borrows the timeline-before-theory discipline
directly, and the proximate-versus-contributing distinction is the same one
week 11 asks you to make when ranking your own findings into a verdict.
