## What this covers

Survivors get written up in trade press as proof a strategy works; nobody
funds a case study of a restaurant that quietly closed in year two. This
lecture makes the argument for reading closures instead: they're better
evidence, not worse, because a business only fails for reasons strong enough
to overcome everyone inside it who wanted it to succeed.

## What it sets up

The slide deck lays out the four analytical lenses the semester returns to
— location, menu, service and narrative — and the discipline of picking one
lens at a time rather than reaching for "it was probably everything" on
week one. Everything from the specimen-selection rules in week 1's session
onward assumes you've sat through this.
