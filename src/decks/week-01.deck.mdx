# Closure Studies

Post-mortems for restaurants that died

---

## Every trade press profile is survivorship bias

- the restaurant that made it: profiled, franchised, written up
- the one three doors down that closed in year two: nothing
- same neighbourhood, same rent, same year — only one leaves evidence

---

{/* _class: quote */}

> A business only fails for reasons strong enough to overcome everyone
> inside it who wanted it to succeed.

---

## A closed restaurant still leaves a trail

- reviews, dated and often specific about what changed near the end
- a delivery-app menu, sometimes still online
- health-inspection records, public in most jurisdictions
- a map pin still marked "permanently closed"

This course reads that trail like an autopsy.

---

## Four lenses, one at a time

1. **location** — foot traffic, rent, what else is on the block
2. **menu** — cost structure, what the prices actually say
3. **service** — the reviews, read for the point sentiment turned
4. **narrative** — the excuse everyone believed, tested against evidence

---

{/* _class: banner */}

## One rule for the semester

Timeline before theory. Write down what happened and when, before you
decide why.

---

## What's due

- **week 4** — Specimen Dossier: prove the case is real
- **week 9** — Autopsy Report: the written case, cross-examined by a peer
- **week 12** — Coroner's Inquest: defend the verdict, live, under questions
